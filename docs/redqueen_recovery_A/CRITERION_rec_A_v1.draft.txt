EFFECT-SETTING INTERPRETATION (frozen; memo §5 on C:5,16,27-32; D:705):
"1.7%"/"3.4%" mean Var(c r)/Var(eta) over every pair of the graph, where eta is
the noiseless capture predictor, r = raw - P(raw), raw = u_i.v_j, P the
unweighted least-squares additive (rank-0, identity link) projection over all
graph pairs (recovery.additive_projection, 40 sweeps), c solved by the 20-step
fixed point of recovery.py:403-407 and recorded per dataset as energy_check
(recovery.py:440). This is NOT the post-refit energy under the registered
monotone 6x6 baseline and link; no claim is made that the registered map's
post-refit fraction equals these values. Scale of eta: in the LOGIT-LINK
scenarios (every main, sensitivity and numerical scenario and every stress
scenario except the two asym ones) eta is the logit of the intended capture
mean sigma(eta) (bank mode, H:1113), not the latent location. In the ASYM
stress scenarios (r0_asym, r2_034_asym) the intended mean is 0.03+0.77
sigma(eta) (eta = 0 gives 0.415), so eta is not the logit of the intended mean;
their energy fraction is defined, and reported, on the eta scale itself.
